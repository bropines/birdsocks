BirdSocks is an unofficial client for NetBird networks. It runs the NetBird client as a userspace node and gives your apps the network through a local SOCKS5 proxy, so it does not take Android's VPN slot and runs next to another VPN, firewall or ad blocker. When you want every app on the network, a VPN (TUN) mode is one switch away.

It works with NetBird Cloud and with a self-hosted NetBird server.

<b>Networking</b>
• Local SOCKS5 proxy with UDP, an optional username and password, optionally reachable from your local network
• Peers, the networks they route and NetBird's DNS names through the proxy — and the rest of the internet, directly or through an exit node
• A local DNS proxy: NetBird names first, everything else through the network's resolvers
• VPN (TUN) mode: NetBird's ranges or all traffic, with excluded apps
• Peers reach the phone's own services (adb over Wi-Fi, an SSH server, a web server) by its NetBird address
• Publish a local port at a public address through your server's NetBird reverse proxy

<b>Management</b>
• Sign in with a setup key or in the browser; several accounts
• Peers with their connection path, latency and traffic; networks and the exit node
• Session expiry shown in advance and extended in the browser
• Diagnostics: events, logs, access check, packet capture, debug bundle
• Quick Settings tile, start after reboot, birdsocks:// links
• Material 3; English and Russian

<b>Network access</b>
The app talks to the NetBird management server you choose (NetBird Cloud by default), to its signal and relay servers and to your peers. A debug bundle is uploaded to NetBird only when you ask for it.

<b>Requirements</b>
Android 7.0 or newer and an account on NetBird Cloud or on a self-hosted NetBird server. No root.
