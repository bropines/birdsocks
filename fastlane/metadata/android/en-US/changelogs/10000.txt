First release. NetBird as a userspace node: apps reach your network through a local SOCKS5 proxy (UDP too) and a DNS proxy, without taking Android's VPN slot; optional VPN mode for every app.
Sign in to NetBird Cloud or your own server, in the browser or with a setup key; several accounts.
Peers, networks, exit nodes, DNS, Publish, diagnostics with logs, packet capture and debug bundle. Server connection through a proxy or ByeDPI. Backup, Tasker automation, Quick Settings tile.
